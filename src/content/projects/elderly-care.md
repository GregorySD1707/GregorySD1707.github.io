- Identified the caregiver, not the patient, as the real end-user through contextual observation and workshops with older adults and caregivers at a Quito geriatric center, redefining the design direction.

- Designed **4 core screens** in Figma: patient detail, triage history, vital signs entry (caregiver), and notification panel (medic).

- Reduced task completion time and eliminated navigation errors detected during usability tests across iterative versions, measured with real caregivers and medics.
